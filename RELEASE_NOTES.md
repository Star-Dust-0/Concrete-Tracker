## Concrete Tracker 1.1.0

Calculates when concrete cubes must be tested, and keeps a local log of your batches.

**Features**
- Casting date to test date for 7, 14, 28 and 56 day tests
- Date difference between any two dates (days, weeks, months, years)
- Saved batches with project name, search, filters (all, upcoming, completed) and share as text
- Arabic and English, follows the system language (RTL supported)
- Light and dark theme, One UI look

Install the APK on Android 7.0 or newer.
